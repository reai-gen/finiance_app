Three everyday ideas to make the world of finance feel more familiar.

01 / EVERYDAY MONEY

Understand your budget

A budget brings money coming in and money going out into one picture.

Explore the basics

Income is money you receive. Expenses are money you spend. The difference shows what is left over, or where spending exceeds income. A monthly view can help you spot recurring costs.

02 / FUTURE PLANS

Give savings a purpose

A savings goal connects an amount of money to something you want to work towards.

Explore the basics

A target amount, what you have already saved, and a regular contribution are the building blocks of a savings plan. Try the calculator below to explore a simple timeline.

03 / THE BIGGER PICTURE

Get to know investing

Investing means putting money into assets with the aim of earning a return over time.

Explore the basics

Returns are uncertain: an investment can fall in value as well as rise. Risk, fees and the length of time money is invested are useful concepts to understand before exploring individual investments.
